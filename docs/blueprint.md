# **App Name**: SORO Landing Pages

## Core Features:

- Landing Page 1: VSL Opt-in: A landing page with a Video Sales Letter (VSL) that captures leads through a form submission, offering a free consultation or demo in exchange for contact information.
- Landing Page 2: Personalized Demo: A landing page offering a personalized demo of SORO, featuring a clear value proposition, customer testimonials, and a direct call to action to schedule the demo. A form is included to collect relevant information for demo personalization.
- Landing Page 3: Lead Magnet Download: Landing page focused on offering a valuable lead magnet (e.g., an ebook on 'Optimizing Dental Clinic Communication') in exchange for contact information. Includes a concise description of the lead magnet's benefits and a simple signup form.
- AI-Powered Chatbot Integration: Integrate a lightweight version of the SORO chatbot (using Gemini) directly on the landing pages to answer initial visitor queries and guide them towards conversion goals. This acts as a tool to pre-qualify leads and address common questions.
- Dynamic Content Personalization: Dynamically adjust the landing page content based on visitor data (e.g., location, referral source) to increase relevance and engagement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Teal/cyan (#2dd4bf) to represent technology, health, and trust, matching the existing brand.
- Background color: Dark blue (#111827) for a sophisticated, modern look, particularly in sections highlighting SORO's technology.
- Accent color: A vibrant purple (#A349A4), used sparingly for call-to-action buttons and highlighting key benefits, to draw attention and create a sense of innovation.
- Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all text, maintaining brand consistency and ensuring readability across devices.
- Use clean, modern icons related to dental care, automation, and communication to visually represent SORO's features and benefits.
- Employ a clear, single-column layout for mobile responsiveness, with distinct sections for headlines, value propositions, testimonials, and forms.
- Use subtle animations to highlight key elements, such as a smooth scroll to the contact form or a progress bar during the VSL, to engage visitors without overwhelming them.
